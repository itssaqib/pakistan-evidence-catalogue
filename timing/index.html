Finding treatment dates

The binding constraint on most of these ideas is not the question, it is knowing which district was treated when. Nine routes, roughly in order of reliability, with what each actually yields.

The practical order

Start with the published evaluation or World Bank ISR sequence — someone may already have built the file. Then ADP scheme registers, which are public and under-used. Then an RTI request naming a specific register. Treat press archives as triangulation only, and verify everything against a primary document before it goes into a regression.

Routes to district-by-date treatment data
SourceWhat it gives you WherePractical note
Provincial gazette notifications The legally authoritative record of when a district was created, when a law was extended, when a facility was notified. Punjab, Sindh, KP and Balochistan each publish a gazette; back issues are held by provincial law departments and the Punjab series is partly digitised. punjablaws.gov.pk Slow but definitive. Use it to settle disagreements between secondary sources, not as your first stop.
Annual Development Programme scheme registers Every publicly funded scheme — a rescue station, a road package, a filtration plant, a hospital upgrade — appears as a line item with a district, an approval date and a completion status. This is the most under-used treatment-timing source in Pakistan. pnd.punjab.gov.pk Published annually as PDFs. Scraping ten years of ADP books yields a district–scheme–date panel for dozens of programmes at once.
PC-1 approval and PC-4 completion documents The PC-1 states the intended district phasing; the PC-4 records what was actually completed and when. Held by the Planning Commission and provincial P&D departments. pc.gov.pk Request through the department. The PC-4 is the one that matters, because plans slip.
Right to Information requests KP (2013), Punjab (2013), Sindh (2016) and Balochistan (2021) all have RTI statutes with statutory response deadlines. A request naming the exact register you want — for example, the date of operationalisation of each Arazi Record Centre — is often the fastest route to a clean treatment-timing file. infocom.punjab.gov.pk Ask for a specific existing record, not for analysis. Requests that ask an official to compile something new are usually refused.
Programme management information systems BISP/NSER, Sehat Sahulat, the Punjab Land Records Authority, PITB, the Punjab Education Foundation, PSDF, and the provincial EMIS and DHIS systems all hold unit-level records with dates. Access runs through a research agreement or data-sharing MoU, not a public download. See the dataset catalogue A World Bank, J-PAL or university affiliation materially improves the odds. Budget three to six months.
World Bank and ADB project documents Project Appraisal Documents, numbered Implementation Status Reports and Implementation Completion Reports record the actual district phasing of donor-financed programmes, often in an annex. documents.worldbank.org The ISR sequence is especially useful: successive reports show the rollout progressing, which effectively date-stamps it.
Third-party and process evaluations Evaluation series and published papers frequently contain the district rollout table as an appendix. Malik, van de Poel & van Doorslaer (2017) is the clearest example: they assembled the health-unit contracting rollout dates across 75 districts. opml.co.uk Always check the replication package before assembling a rollout file from scratch.
Press archives Dawn, The News, Express Tribune and Business Recorder report launch ceremonies with dates and district lists. Some programme phases are documented almost exclusively this way. dawn.com/archive Useful for triangulation, unreliable alone — a launch ceremony is not an operational start.
Satellite and crowd-sourced verification Where administrative dates are unavailable or untrustworthy, nightlights, OpenStreetMap edit history and mobile coverage polygons can date the arrival of infrastructure independently. eogdata.mines.edu Best used to validate an administrative date rather than to replace it.

Drafting an RTI request that works

The commonest reason a request fails is that it asks an official to produce analysis rather than hand over a record that already exists. Name the register, the custodian and the fields.

To: Public Information Officer, [Department]
Under Section 10, Punjab Transparency and
Right to Information Act 2013

I request a copy of the following existing record:

1. The register or database recording the date on
   which each [facility / centre / scheme] under
   [programme name] became operational, listing for
   each entry: district, tehsil, name of the unit,
   and date of operationalisation.

2. Covering the period [start] to [end].

I am content to receive this in any electronic
format in which it is already held, including a
spreadsheet export. I do not require any new
compilation or analysis.

How this catalogue grades timing data

  • High — dates are published or exist in a named register that has already been assembled by someone; verification is a matter of hours.
  • Medium-High — dates exist in administrative records and are obtainable with a request or a document search, typically weeks.
  • Medium — dates exist but are scattered across sources that disagree; expect to reconcile and document judgement calls.
  • Low-Medium — no reliable district-level dating known; would require primary data collection or a satellite-based proxy.
