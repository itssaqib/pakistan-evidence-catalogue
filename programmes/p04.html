BISP Waseela-e-Taleem (primary education CCT)

Classic multi-wave district phase-in. Rolled out by district across 5 -> ~32 -> ~50 -> all, launched November 2012 in 5 districts; expanded in waves to ~50 districts by 2016; effectively nationwide by 2020.

Where to get district-by-date data

BISP annual reports and board minutes; Ehsaas policy documents; World Bank project appraisal documents

Assessed quality: High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Textbook staggered adoption. Combine ASER district-year learning and enrolment data with the launch date for a Callaway-Sant'Anna event study.

Main threats to identification

Concurrent provincial stipend schemes; selection of low-enrolment districts implies mean reversion - check pre-trends carefully

Existing evidence

Meta-analysis cited in Ehsaas documents finds enrolment gains but weak attendance and learning effects
