BISP Kafaalat unconditional cash transfer (first payment)

Continuous phase-in of coverage intensity plus discrete district start dates. Rolled out by district across All districts, 2008-09 parliamentarian phase; scorecard-based enrolment 2010-2016; continuous expansion since.

Assessed quality: Medium-High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Two designs stack neatly: (i) staggered district DiD on the first-payment date, (ii) RD at the PMT cut-off within district. The interaction identifies whether the transfer works differently where coverage is dense.

Main threats to identification

General-equilibrium spillovers within districts break SUTVA when coverage is high; inflation eroded the real transfer over time

Existing evidence

OPM final evaluation (2016); Haseeb & Vyborny (2022) on payment mechanisms
