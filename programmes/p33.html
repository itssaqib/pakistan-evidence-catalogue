Punjab Skills Development Fund (PSDF) training rollout

Clear multi-year district phase-in. Rolled out by sub-district across 4 -> 36, four southern districts (Bahawalpur, Bahawalnagar, Lodhran, Rahim Yar Khan) from 2010; expanded to all 36 districts by around 2016.

Status: Open  High timing data

Where to get district-by-date data

PSDF MIS with scheme dates and district lots; PSDF annual reports

Assessed quality: High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

PSDF ran several RCTs, so mechanism evidence exists; the district phase-in scales it up. Trainee-level MIS can be linked to LFS district aggregates.

Main threats to identification

Deliberate targeting of poor districts; training supply concentrated in cities within districts

Existing evidence

Cheema, Khwaja, Naseer & Shapiro on PSDF vouchers and stipends
