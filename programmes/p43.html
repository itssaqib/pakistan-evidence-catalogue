Creation of new districts (district bifurcation)

Staggered creation events. Rolled out by district across ~15-25 splits since 2000, nankana Sahib 2005; Chiniot 2009; Sindh and Balochistan splits through the 2000s-2010s; Punjab created Kot Addu, Talagang, Murree, Wazirabad in 2022 and Taunsa in 2023.

Where to get district-by-date data

Provincial gazette notifications with dates; PBS district code changes

Assessed quality: High for the notification date. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

'Getting your own district' means a new DC, DHQ hospital, district education office and budget line - a discrete jump in local administrative presence. Outcomes: service delivery, public employment, nightlights in the new headquarters town.

Main threats to identification

Splits are politically chosen; the panel must be re-based to constant boundaries (harmonise to pre-split parents) before any analysis

Existing evidence

Almost nothing in the Pakistani literature; a strong, feasible paper
