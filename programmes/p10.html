Ehsaas Interest-Free Loans (Akhuwat partnership)

Sequential opening of loan centres. Rolled out by tehsil across ~130 districts by 2022, district-wise expansion of loan centres 2019-2022.

Status: Open  Medium timing data

Where to get district-by-date data

PPAF and Akhuwat progress reports; Ehsaas dashboards

Assessed quality: Medium. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Combine centre opening dates with PMN MicroWatch quarterly district penetration to measure crowd-in or crowd-out of commercial microfinance.

Main threats to identification

Akhuwat's pre-existing footprint is correlated with district development

Existing evidence

Limited published evaluation

Outcomes and data (4)

Each row pairs an outcome with the mechanism that would produce it, the expected direction, and a dataset that measures it at a compatible resolution. Expected signs are hypotheses, not findings.

O-FIN-02 Access to credit Financial inclusion New zero-interest loan supply + MicroWatch (microfinance outreach); PSLM / HIES (Pakistan Social & Living Standards Measurement Survey); Banking Statistics of Pakistan / district-wise credit & deposits PMN quarterly district penetration is the natural outcome series
O-FIN-03 Commercial microfinance penetration Financial inclusion Subsidised credit may crowd out commercial MFIs - (crowd-out) or + (market building) MicroWatch (microfinance outreach) A genuinely open empirical question with policy stakes
O-LAB-08 Self-employment and microenterprise ownership Labour Loans finance small enterprise entry +, small Labour Force Survey; PSLM / HIES (Pakistan Social & Living Standards Measurement Survey) Microcredit literature suggests modest effects
O-POV-01 Household consumption Poverty Credit smooths consumption +, small PSLM / HIES (Pakistan Social & Living Standards Measurement Survey); Household Integrated Economic Survey (consumption module) Expect small effects consistent with the global microcredit literature
