Rescue 1122 emergency service district expansion

Staggered district-by-district launch over roughly a decade. Rolled out by district across 36 in Punjab; later other provinces, lahore pilot 14 October 2004; phased expansion reaching all 36 Punjab districts by roughly 2010-2015; tehsil-level expansion from 2018.

Treatment timing

Where to get district-by-date data

Punjab Emergency Service annual reports and district pages; Punjab P&D ADP scheme documents (district-wise rescue station schemes)

Assessed quality: Medium-High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

A rare public-service treatment with a hard, dated switch-on and a mechanically linked outcome (response time, trauma mortality). Also usable as a first stage for the effect of emergency care on road-accident mortality.

Main threats to identification

Roads and vehicle fleets expanded simultaneously; crash reporting improves precisely because the service exists (measurement endogeneity)

Existing evidence

Descriptive Rescue 1122 statistics; almost no causal work

Outcomes and data (6)

Each row pairs an outcome with the mechanism that would produce it, the expected direction, and a dataset that measures it at a compatible resolution. Expected signs are hypotheses, not findings.
