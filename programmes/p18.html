    Rescue 1122

    Rescue 1122 emergency service district expansion. Lahore pilot 14 October 2004; phased expansion reaching all 36 Punjab districts by roughly 2010-2015; tehsil-level expansion from 2018.

    Overview

    A rare public-service treatment with a hard, dated switch-on and a mechanically linked outcome (response time, trauma mortality). Also usable as a first stage for the effect of emergency care on road-accident mortality.

    Implementing agency
    Punjab Emergency Service Department
    Scope
    Punjab (then replicated in KP, Sindh, Balochistan, GB, AJK)
    Rollout window
    Lahore pilot 14 October 2004; phased expansion reaching all 36 Punjab districts by roughly 2010-2015; tehsil-level expansion from 2018
    Unit of rollout
    District (service launch date)
    Type of staggering
    Staggered district-by-district launch over roughly a decade
    Assignment rule
    Sequencing driven by budget releases and political priority; the third-party evaluation of the pilot triggered scale-up
    Designs it suits
    Staggered DiD, Instrumental variables

    Rollout

    1. 2004PilotLahore. Punjab Emergency Service — Emergency Ambulance Service
    2. 2015Remaining Punjab districtsAll Punjab districts by about 2015; the year is approximate. Coverage in other provinces is not dated here. Express Tribune — Rescue 1122 will expand to Punjab tehsils by 2019
    Where to get district-by-date data

    Punjab Emergency Service annual reports and district pages; Punjab P&D ADP scheme documents (district-wise rescue station schemes)

    Assessed quality: Medium-High. How to obtain timing data

    Identification

    Main threats

    Roads and vehicle fleets expanded simultaneously; crash reporting improves precisely because the service exists (measurement endogeneity)

    Existing evidence

    Descriptive Rescue 1122 statistics; almost no causal work

    Data coverage

    Survey waves before, during and after the rollout window (2004–2018, shaded). Waves before the first phase identify pre-trends; waves inside the window carry the staggered comparison.

    Power

    Constant 2019 boundaries in scope
    0.5 suits a not-yet-treated comparison
    AR(1) residual correlation
    —
    minimum detectable effect, in residual standard deviations

    Difference-in-differences variance with serially correlated errors (Burlig, Preonas and Woerman 2020), AR(1) residuals, two-group equivalent. Heterogeneity-robust staggered estimators usually need somewhat more.
