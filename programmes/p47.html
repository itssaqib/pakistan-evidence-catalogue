Assessed quality: Medium-High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Use distance to the nearest interchange rather than 'district crossed' - the treatment is access, not adjacency. A least-cost-path counterfactual route is the standard instrument.

Main threats to identification

Routes are chosen to connect places already growing; the classic endogenous-placement problem

Existing evidence

Some CPEC descriptive work; rigorous Pakistani road-effect papers are scarce

Outcomes and data (6)

Each row pairs an outcome with the mechanism that would produce it, the expected direction, and a dataset that measures it at a compatible resolution. Expected signs are hypotheses, not findings.
