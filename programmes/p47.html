    P47   Infrastructure

    Motorway expansion

    Motorway and expressway network expansion (incl. CPEC corridors). M-1 2007, M-4 sections 2015-2019, M-5 Multan-Sukkur 2019, M-11 Lahore-Sialkot 2020, M-14 Hakla-DI Khan 2021.

    Overview

    Use distance to the nearest interchange rather than 'district crossed' - the treatment is access, not adjacency. A least-cost-path counterfactual route is the standard instrument.

    Implementing agency
    National Highway Authority; provincial highway departments
    Scope
    National
    Rollout window
    M-1 2007, M-4 sections 2015-2019, M-5 Multan-Sukkur 2019, M-11 Lahore-Sialkot 2020, M-14 Hakla-DI Khan 2021
    Unit of rollout
    Road section, mapped to districts crossed and interchange locations
    Type of staggering
    Staggered section openings with recorded dates
    Assignment rule
    Route selection is political and topographic
    Designs it suits
    Staggered DiD, Instrumental variables

    Rollout

    No district-by-date list has been documented for this entry yet. The map shows its stated scope only.

    Where to get district-by-date data

    NHA project completion records; CPEC portal; OSM historical extracts

    Assessed quality: Medium-High. How to obtain timing data

    Open this programme in the atlas

    Identification

    Main threats

    Routes are chosen to connect places already growing; the classic endogenous-placement problem

    Existing evidence

    Some CPEC descriptive work; rigorous Pakistani road-effect papers are scarce

    Survey waves before, during and after the rollout window (2002–2021, shaded). Waves before the first phase identify pre-trends; waves inside the window carry the staggered comparison.

    Power

    Constant 2019 boundaries in scope
    0.5 suits a not-yet-treated comparison
    AR(1) residual correlation
    —
    minimum detectable effect, in residual standard deviations

    Difference-in-differences variance with serially correlated errors (Burlig, Preonas and Woerman 2020), AR(1) residuals, two-group equivalent. Heterogeneity-robust staggered estimators usually need somewhat more.
