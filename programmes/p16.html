    Contracting out health units

    Contracting out of Basic Health Units (PRSP / PPHI). Rahim Yar Khan pilot 2003; expansion to 82-83 districts across four provinces 2005-2013.

    Overview

    Already-collected district roll-out dates exist in the published literature - a large fixed cost is already paid. Re-analyse with modern staggered estimators and richer outcomes (DHIS, MICS) than the original paper used.

    Implementing agency
    Provincial governments with Rural Support Programmes (PRSP, SRSO, SRSP, NRSP)
    Scope
    National
    Rollout window
    Rahim Yar Khan pilot 2003; expansion to 82-83 districts across four provinces 2005-2013
    Unit of rollout
    District (contract signing / handover date)
    Type of staggering
    Staggered handover of the district's BHU network to an RSP
    Assignment rule
    Provincial decisions; documented district-level roll-out dates
    Designs it suits
    Staggered DiD, Treatment switches off

    Rollout

    1. 2003PRSP pilotRahim Yar Khan. Public Health (2019) — PPP in health service delivery in Sindh
    2. 2007First district in SindhKashmore. PPHI Sindh — organisation profile (started in Kashmore)
    3. LaterFurther districtsLater phases reached more districts; their dates need the timing source below.
    Where to get district-by-date data

    Malik, van de Poel & van Doorslaer (2017) assembled the district roll-out dates from PPHI/CMPHI administration; provincial health department notifications

    Assessed quality: High. How to obtain timing data

    Identification

    Main threats

    Contracting was often reversed or renegotiated (Punjab took BHUs back and re-contracted through PHFMC); treatment is not absorbing

    Existing evidence

    Malik, van de Poel & van Doorslaer (2017, Health Policy & Planning); Loevinsohn et al. on Rahim Yar Khan

    References

    • Malik, M. A., Van de Poel, E. and Van Doorslaer, E. (2017). Did contracting effect the use of primary health care units in Pakistan?. Health Policy and Planning 32(7), 1032–1041. Link

    Survey waves before, during and after the rollout window (2003–2013, shaded). Waves before the first phase identify pre-trends; waves inside the window carry the staggered comparison.

    Power

    Constant 2019 boundaries in scope
    0.5 suits a not-yet-treated comparison
    AR(1) residual correlation
    —
    minimum detectable effect, in residual standard deviations

    Difference-in-differences variance with serially correlated errors (Burlig, Preonas and Woerman 2020), AR(1) residuals, two-group equivalent. Heterogeneity-robust staggered estimators usually need somewhat more.
