Identification

The eligibility rule (a verified digital land record) makes LRMIS completion a first stage for Kisan Card access. That is an unusually clean two-step design.

Main threats to identification

Only landowners are eligible, so tenants are mechanically excluded - a distributional outcome in itself

Existing evidence

None known
