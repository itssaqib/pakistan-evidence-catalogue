    Punjab Kisan Card

    Punjab Kisan Card / e-Credit Scheme. District-wise enrolment of farmers from 2018-19; relaunched 2024-25.

    Overview

    The eligibility rule (a verified digital land record) makes LRMIS completion a first stage for Kisan Card access. That is an unusually clean two-step design.

    Implementing agency
    Agriculture Department, Punjab with Bank of Punjab
    Scope
    Punjab
    Rollout window
    District-wise enrolment of farmers from 2018-19; relaunched 2024-25
    Unit of rollout
    District (enrolment drive dates)
    Type of staggering
    Phase-in
    Assignment rule
    Land records verification through LRMIS - so eligibility is mechanically linked to digitisation
    Designs it suits
    Staggered DiD, Instrumental variables

    Rollout

    No district-by-date list has been documented for this entry yet. The map shows its stated scope only.

    Where to get district-by-date data

    Punjab Agriculture Department and Bank of Punjab MIS; LRMIS linkage records

    Assessed quality: Medium-High. How to obtain timing data

    Identification

    Main threats

    Only landowners are eligible, so tenants are mechanically excluded - a distributional outcome in itself

    Existing evidence

    None known

    Data coverage

    Survey waves before, during and after the rollout window (2018–2024, shaded). Waves before the first phase identify pre-trends; waves inside the window carry the staggered comparison.

    Power

    Constant 2019 boundaries in scope
    0.5 suits a not-yet-treated comparison
    AR(1) residual correlation
    —
    minimum detectable effect, in residual standard deviations

    Difference-in-differences variance with serially correlated errors (Burlig, Preonas and Woerman 2020), AR(1) residuals, two-group equivalent. Heterogeneity-robust staggered estimators usually need somewhat more.
