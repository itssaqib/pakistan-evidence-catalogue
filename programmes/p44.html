Right to Information legislation

Province-level staggering only. Rolled out by province across 4 provinces, kP and Punjab 2013; Sindh 2016; Balochistan 2021; federal 2017.

Where to get district-by-date data

Provincial acts with commencement dates; information commission annual reports

Assessed quality: High for dates, but the unit is coarse. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Four treated units at province level is too few for a district DiD, but the RTI request data themselves (requests filed per district) give a district-level compliance outcome.

Main threats to identification

Only four units; enforcement varies enormously from the statute
