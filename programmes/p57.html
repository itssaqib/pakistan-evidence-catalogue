    Model Bazaars

    Punjab Model Bazaars / Sahulat Bazaars. Bazaars opened district by district from 2011.

    Overview

    Directly testable on retail price outcomes using PBS city price data and AMIS mandi prices around opening dates.

    Implementing agency
    Punjab Model Bazaars Management Company
    Scope
    Punjab
    Rollout window
    Bazaars opened district by district from 2011
    Unit of rollout
    District / city (bazaar opening date)
    Type of staggering
    Sequential openings
    Assignment rule
    Political and urban prioritisation
    Designs it suits
    Staggered DiD

    Rollout

    No district-by-date list has been documented for this entry yet. The map shows its stated scope only.

    Where to get district-by-date data

    PMBMC bazaar registers with opening dates; Punjab ADP

    Assessed quality: Medium. How to obtain timing data

    Identification

    Main threats

    Bazaars are urban and few; price effects may be local to a neighbourhood

    Existing evidence

    None known

    Outcomes and data

    Each row pairs an outcome with the mechanism that would move it, the direction expected, and a dataset at a compatible resolution. Directions are hypotheses, not findings.

    Data coverage

    Survey waves before, during and after the rollout window (2011, shaded). Waves before the first phase identify pre-trends; waves inside the window carry the staggered comparison.

    Survey waveContinuous seriesRollout window

    Power

    Constant 2019 boundaries in scope
    0.5 suits a not-yet-treated comparison
    AR(1) residual correlation
    —
    minimum detectable effect, in residual standard deviations

    Difference-in-differences variance with serially correlated errors (Burlig, Preonas and Woerman 2020), AR(1) residuals, two-group equivalent. Heterogeneity-robust staggered estimators usually need somewhat more.
