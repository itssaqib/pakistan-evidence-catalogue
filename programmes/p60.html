National Action Plan and post-APS security surge

Component-by-component and province-by-province variation. Rolled out by district across All districts, announced December 2014-January 2015; components (military courts, madrassa registration, Fourth Schedule enforcement) implemented at different times by province and district.

Assessed quality: Low-Medium. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Poorly documented at district level. Realistically usable only as a national break point unless component-level district records can be obtained.

Main threats to identification

Simultaneous with Zarb-e-Azb; national timing gives no control group

Existing evidence

Descriptive PIPS reporting
