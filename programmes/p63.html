2022 floods and post-flood response

Continuous intensity. Rolled out by district across ~90+ districts, june-October 2022; BISP flood relief payments late 2022.

Assessed quality: High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

PSLM 2019-20 gives a genuine district-representative baseline immediately before the shock - unusually good timing for a DiD.

Main threats to identification

Post-flood survey coverage is limited so far; overlaps with the 2022-23 macroeconomic crisis

Existing evidence

World Bank PDNA; emerging literature

Datasets used here

  • ASER Pakistan (Annual Status of Education Report)Idara-e-Taleem-o-Aagahi (ITA) · Yes - rural district-representative (~140-155 districts)
  • Crop Reporting Service district crop area & yieldAgriculture Department, Punjab / Sindh CRS · Yes
  • Flood inundation products (Sentinel-1 SAR, MODIS MOD09, UNOSAT, Copernicus EMS, DFO)UNOSAT / Copernicus / Dartmouth Flood Observatory · Aggregate to district
  • Humanitarian Data Exchange - Pakistan (boundaries, damage, IDPs)UN OCHA · Yes - canonical admin boundaries
  • MODIS / Sentinel-2 vegetation, land cover and cropland productsNASA / ESA (via Google Earth Engine) · Aggregate to district
