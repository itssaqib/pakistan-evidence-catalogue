FATA-KP merger and extension of the regular legal and administrative system

Phased extension of each institution. Rolled out by agency / tribal district across 7 tribal districts + 6 FRs, 25th Amendment May 2018; police (Levies conversion), courts, and provincial departments extended in phases 2018-2021; first provincial assembly elections July 2019.

Identification

One of the most interesting state-capacity natural experiments anywhere: formal courts and police replaced the FCR jirga system on datable schedules. The KP MICS 2019 covers merged districts.

Main threats to identification

Almost no pre-merger household data exist for FATA; simultaneous military operations and IDP returns; population figures are contested

Existing evidence

Very thin - a major open area, and a natural fit for a ReCIPE-style research agenda
