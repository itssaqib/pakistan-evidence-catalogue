    P42   Governance & state capacity

    FATA–KP merger

    FATA-KP merger and extension of the regular legal and administrative system. 25th Amendment May 2018; police (Levies conversion), courts, and provincial departments extended in phases 2018-2021; first provincial assembly elections July 2019.

    Overview

    One of the most interesting state-capacity natural experiments anywhere: formal courts and police replaced the FCR jirga system on datable schedules. The KP MICS 2019 covers merged districts.

    Implementing agency
    Federal and KP governments
    Scope
    Ex-FATA (7 tribal districts, 6 frontier regions)
    Rollout window
    25th Amendment May 2018; police (Levies conversion), courts, and provincial departments extended in phases 2018-2021; first provincial assembly elections July 2019
    Unit of rollout
    Tribal district / frontier region
    Type of staggering
    Phased extension of each institution
    Assignment rule
    Constitutional change, then phased administrative extension
    Designs it suits
    Staggered DiD

    Rollout

    1. 2018Merged tribal districtsBajaur, Mohmand, Khyber, Kurram, Orakzai, North Waziristan, South Waziristan. Constitution (Twenty-fifth Amendment) Act, 2018
    Where to get district-by-date data

    25th Amendment; KP notifications extending each law and department with dates; ECP 2019 results

    Assessed quality: Medium-High. How to obtain timing data

    Identification

    Main threats

    Almost no pre-merger household data exist for FATA; simultaneous military operations and IDP returns; population figures are contested

    Existing evidence

    Very thin - a major open area, and a natural fit for a ReCIPE-style research agenda

    Power

    Constant 2019 boundaries in scope
    From the documented district list
    AR(1) residual correlation
    —
    minimum detectable effect, in residual standard deviations

    Difference-in-differences variance with serially correlated errors (Burlig, Preonas and Woerman 2020), AR(1) residuals, two-group equivalent. Heterogeneity-robust staggered estimators usually need somewhat more.
