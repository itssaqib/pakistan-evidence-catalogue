Punjab Land Records Management Information System (LRMIS) / Arazi Record Centres

Sharp tehsil-by-tehsil operationalisation with recorded dates. Rolled out by tehsil across 36 districts, 143 tehsil centres, service centres operationalised 5 (2011), 26 (2012), 100 (2013), 133 (2014); all 143 centres across 36 districts by 2016-17.

Identification

The best-documented staggered e-government reform in Pakistan, and it operates at tehsil level - finer than district, so within-district variation identifies the effect free of district-level political confounds. Outcomes: mutation volumes, dispute filings, agricultural credit, land prices.

Main threats to identification

Digitisation order correlates with pre-existing record quality; the patwari system persisted in parallel for years; the 2017 PLRA reorganisation is a second, separate event

Existing evidence

Hussain & Ullah (2023, Land Use Policy); World Bank feature reporting; the credit and enforcement spillovers remain under-studied
