    Punjab land records (LRMIS)

    Punjab Land Records Management Information System (LRMIS) / Arazi Record Centres. Service centres operationalised 5 (2011), 26 (2012), 100 (2013), 133 (2014); all 143 centres across 36 districts by 2016-17.

    Overview

    The best-documented staggered e-government reform in Pakistan, and it operates at tehsil level - finer than district, so within-district variation identifies the effect free of district-level political confounds. Outcomes: mutation volumes, dispute filings, agricultural credit, land prices.

    Implementing agency
    Punjab Board of Revenue with the World Bank and PITB
    Scope
    Punjab
    Rollout window
    Service centres operationalised 5 (2011), 26 (2012), 100 (2013), 133 (2014); all 143 centres across 36 districts by 2016-17
    Unit of rollout
    Tehsil (ARC), aggregable to district
    Type of staggering
    Sharp tehsil-by-tehsil operationalisation with recorded dates
    Assignment rule
    Sequencing by scanning and data-entry completion for each district's records
    Designs it suits
    Staggered DiD

    No district-by-date list has been documented for this entry yet. The map shows its stated scope only.

    Where to get district-by-date data

    Identification

    Main threats

    Digitisation order correlates with pre-existing record quality; the patwari system persisted in parallel for years; the 2017 PLRA reorganisation is a second, separate event

    Existing evidence

    Hussain & Ullah (2023, Land Use Policy); World Bank feature reporting; the credit and enforcement spillovers remain under-studied

    References

    • KDI School of Public Policy and Management (2021). Impact evaluation of the Land Record Management Information System in the Punjab Province, Pakistan. KDI School Working Paper DP21-12. Link
    • EGOV-CeDEM-ePart 2020 (conference paper) (2020). The e-governance of land record and social dispute resolution: An impact evaluation of the Punjab Land Record Management Information System. CEUR Workshop Proceedings, Vol. 2797. Link

    Power

    Constant 2019 boundaries in scope
    0.5 suits a not-yet-treated comparison
    AR(1) residual correlation
    —
    minimum detectable effect, in residual standard deviations

    Difference-in-differences variance with serially correlated errors (Burlig, Preonas and Woerman 2020), AR(1) residuals, two-group equivalent. Heterogeneity-robust staggered estimators usually need somewhat more.
