    P17   Health

    PHFMC outsourcing

    Punjab Health Facility Management Company outsourcing (second wave). Low-performing districts first (Nankana Sahib, Bahawalnagar, Narowal, Mandi Bahauddin), then wider expansion 2016-2019.

    Overview

    Facility-level assignment inside districts allows a within-district design that removes district-level political confounds - much stronger than a pure district DiD.

    Implementing agency
    Punjab Health Department / PHFMC
    Scope
    Punjab
    Rollout window
    Low-performing districts first (Nankana Sahib, Bahawalnagar, Narowal, Mandi Bahauddin), then wider expansion 2016-2019
    Unit of rollout
    District / facility
    Type of staggering
    Phase-in, with facility-level assignment inside districts
    Assignment rule
    Explicitly targeted at low-performing districts and facilities
    Designs it suits
    Staggered DiD

    Rollout

    1. 2015First districts identifiedNankana Sahib, Bahawalnagar, Narowal, Mandi Bahauddin. Dawn — Punjab to 'outsource' basic health project (14 May 2015)
    2. LaterFurther districtsLater phases reached more districts; their dates need the timing source below.
    Where to get district-by-date data

    PHFMC annual reports; Punjab Health Department notifications; Dawn coverage of the phased plan

    Assessed quality: Medium-High. How to obtain timing data

    Main threats

    Selection on low performance guarantees mean reversion; contract terms changed across waves

    Existing evidence

    Limited published evaluation

    Survey waves before, during and after the rollout window (2015–2019, shaded). Waves before the first phase identify pre-trends; waves inside the window carry the staggered comparison.

    Power

    Constant 2019 boundaries in scope
    0.5 suits a not-yet-treated comparison
    AR(1) residual correlation
    —
    minimum detectable effect, in residual standard deviations

    Difference-in-differences variance with serially correlated errors (Burlig, Preonas and Woerman 2020), AR(1) residuals, two-group equivalent. Heterogeneity-robust staggered estimators usually need somewhat more.
