Punjab Public School Support Programme (PSSP) / PEIMA outsourced public schools

Phase-in by lot. Rolled out by sub-district across Bahawalpur, Chiniot, Rahim Yar Khan, Muzaffargarh, Kasur and others, phased handover of low-enrolment public schools to private operators, by district lots, 2016-2019.

Where to get district-by-date data

PEIMA school registers with handover dates; ITA and other operator reports

Assessed quality: Medium-High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

School-level handover dates with an enrolment-threshold eligibility rule support both an RD and a staggered DiD, with within-district controls.

Main threats to identification

Selection on low enrolment implies mean reversion; operator quality varies

Existing evidence

Some World Bank and operator reporting; thin independent evidence
