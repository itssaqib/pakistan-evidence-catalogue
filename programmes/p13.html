Multi-phase district activation. Rolled out by district across 34 districts in 2015 -> 67 by 2019, launched 31 December 2015 in an initial district set; SSP-II reached 67 districts by 2019.

Where to get district-by-date data

SSP MIS and provincial health department notifications; Sehat Sahulat programme history page; NADRA enrolment records

Assessed quality: High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

The strongest health-finance staggered design in Pakistan. Pair district activation dates with HIES out-of-pocket spending and DHIS/SLIC claims. The within-district PMT cut-off gives a complementary RD.

Main threats to identification

Empanelled hospitals are concentrated in cities, so effective access varies within district; supply response (new private hospitals) is itself an outcome

Existing evidence

Descriptive reviews only (Khan et al. KMUJ 2022; MDPI IJERPH 2022); the causal literature is essentially open

Outcomes and data (7)

Each row pairs an outcome with the mechanism that would produce it, the expected direction, and a dataset that measures it at a compatible resolution. Expected signs are hypotheses, not findings.
