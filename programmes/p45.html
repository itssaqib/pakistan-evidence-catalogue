3G/4G spectrum award and mobile broadband coverage expansion

Continuous coverage expansion with district-specific arrival dates. Rolled out by district across All districts, spectrum auctioned April 2014; operator coverage expanded district by district 2014-2020; 4G reached ~95% of networks by 2025.

Where to get district-by-date data

GSMA/Collins Bartholomew Mobile Coverage Explorer annual polygons; OpenCellID; PTA annual reports; Ookla from 2019

Assessed quality: Medium (commercial data are licensed). Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Coverage arrival dates give a strong staggered design, but rollout is commercially endogenous. Instrument with terrain ruggedness, distance to fibre backbone, or use the USF-subsidised lots (P46) as the exogenous component.

Main threats to identification

Endogenous commercial placement is the central threat; smartphone affordability moved simultaneously

Existing evidence

Some work on mobile money in Pakistan; district broadband effects are largely unstudied

Each row pairs an outcome with the mechanism that would produce it, the expected direction, and a dataset that measures it at a compatible resolution. Expected signs are hypotheses, not findings.
