Assessed quality: High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Uses a sharp national date interacted with continuous district exposure - avoids the endogenous-placement problem entirely.

Main threats to identification

Exposure intensity is itself correlated with district characteristics; requires strong pre-trend evidence

Existing evidence

Hussain & Yoon (2025, Labour Economics) - terrorism and education
