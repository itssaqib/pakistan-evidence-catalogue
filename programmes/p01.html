P01 · Social protection · Programme

BISP Poverty Scorecard census (NSER) rollout

Phase-in: districts entered the poverty census on different dates because multiple third-party firms worked in parallel. Rolled out by district across ~130 districts + AJK/GB, 2009 test phase (16 districts) then national rollout 2010-2011.

Where to get district-by-date data

BISP/NSER MIS district census start and completion dates; RSPN and OPM evaluation annexes; ADB and World Bank project documents

Assessed quality: Medium-High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Cleanest large-scale staggered treatment in Pakistan. Use the district census completion date as the event date: eligibility, and hence cash, could only begin once a district was surveyed. OPM's evaluation explicitly notes payments began in some districts before the census had finished in others.

Main threats to identification

Firms may have been assigned easier or nearer districts first; 2010 floods interrupted fieldwork in Sindh and south Punjab; concurrent flood cash grants (Watan Card)

Existing evidence

OPM/Oxford Policy Management BISP impact evaluation series (2011-2016); Cheema et al.; Ambler & De Brauw (2017)
