    P01   Social protection

    BISP poverty census rollout

    BISP Poverty Scorecard census (NSER) rollout. 2009 test phase (16 districts) then national rollout 2010-2011.

    Overview

    Cleanest large-scale staggered treatment in Pakistan. Use the district census completion date as the event date: eligibility, and hence cash, could only begin once a district was surveyed. OPM's evaluation explicitly notes payments began in some districts before the census had finished in others.

    Implementing agency
    Benazir Income Support Programme with RSPN, PCO and survey firms
    Scope
    National
    Rollout window
    2009 test phase (16 districts) then national rollout 2010-2011
    Unit of rollout
    District (census completion date)
    Type of staggering
    Phase-in: districts entered the poverty census on different dates because multiple third-party firms worked in parallel
    Assignment rule
    Administrative sequencing by contracted survey firm and cluster; not random but plausibly unrelated to short-run outcomes
    Designs it suits
    Staggered DiD

    Identification

    Main threats

    Firms may have been assigned easier or nearer districts first; 2010 floods interrupted fieldwork in Sindh and south Punjab; concurrent flood cash grants (Watan Card)

    Existing evidence

    OPM/Oxford Policy Management BISP impact evaluation series (2011-2016); Cheema et al.; Ambler & De Brauw (2017)

    References

    • Oxford Policy Management (2016). Benazir Income Support Programme: Final impact evaluation report. OPM, Oxford. Link

    • Ambler, K. and de Brauw, A. (2017). The impacts of cash transfers on women's empowerment: Learning from Pakistan's BISP program. World Bank Social Protection & Labor Discussion Paper 1702. Find on Google Scholar (details to verify)

    Power

    Constant 2019 boundaries in scope
    0.5 suits a not-yet-treated comparison
    AR(1) residual correlation
    —
    minimum detectable effect, in residual standard deviations

    Difference-in-differences variance with serially correlated errors (Burlig, Preonas and Woerman 2020), AR(1) residuals, two-group equivalent. Heterogeneity-robust staggered estimators usually need somewhat more.
