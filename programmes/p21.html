EPI strengthening, IPV introduction and polio campaign intensification

High-frequency continuous variation in campaign rounds rather than a single switch. Rolled out by district across All districts, varying intensity, iPV introduced 2015; district risk classification and campaign intensity vary by round from 2014.

Treatment timing

Where to get district-by-date data

EOC campaign records; WHO LQAS reports; DHIS immunisation module

Assessed quality: Medium. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Very high frequency (campaign rounds every few weeks) supports precise event studies for routine immunisation spillovers.

Main threats to identification

Deliberate targeting of high-risk districts; security incidents against vaccinators are correlated with conflict outcomes

Existing evidence

Large public health literature; little economics
