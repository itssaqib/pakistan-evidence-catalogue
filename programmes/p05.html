Ehsaas Taleemi Wazaif (redesigned education CCT)

Phase-in by district as biometric enrolment desks were established. Rolled out by district across All districts, district-wise digital rollout 2020-2022 replacing Waseela-e-Taleem.

Assessed quality: Medium. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Difference from P04 is the digitised compliance monitoring. Treat the switch as a separate event and test whether the redesign added value over the paper-based scheme.

Main threats to identification

Overlaps with COVID school closures - the confound is severe in 2020-21

Existing evidence

Limited independent evaluation to date
