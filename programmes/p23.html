PEF Foundation Assisted Schools (FAS)

Ten documented expansion phases. Rolled out by sub-district across 6 -> all 36, launched 2005 in 6 districts; ten expansion phases to province-wide coverage.

Where to get district-by-date data

PEF phase notifications and partner school registers with partnership dates; PEF annual reports

Assessed quality: High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Two levels of variation: district phase entry and school-level QAT pass/fail (the QAT score threshold is a genuine RD). Barrera-Osorio & Raju exploited the school-level cut-off; the district phase-in is still open.

Main threats to identification

Partner schools are self-selected private schools; displacement of public enrolment is a real outcome, not a nuisance

Existing evidence

Barrera-Osorio & Raju (2015, 2017) on the FAS QAT discontinuity; ADB (2010) case study
