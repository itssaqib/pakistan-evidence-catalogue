    KP Sehat Card Plus

    KP Sehat Card Plus (universal provincial coverage). Four pilot districts from December 2015 (Malakand, Kohat, Mardan, Chitral; poorest ~21% of households); ~24 districts by 2017; all districts and 100% of the population from 20 August 2020.

    Overview

    Two treatments in one: extensive margin (district entry) and intensive margin (share of population covered rising from 21% to 100%). A dose-response event study is feasible.

    Implementing agency
    KP Health Department with SLIC
    Scope
    Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
    Rollout window
    Four pilot districts from December 2015 (Malakand, Kohat, Mardan, Chitral; poorest ~21% of households); ~24 districts by 2017; all districts and 100% of the population from 20 August 2020
    Unit of rollout
    District (phase entry)
    Type of staggering
    Four documented phases with expanding population and benefit coverage
    Assignment rule
    Pilot districts chosen administratively; later expansion political and universal
    Designs it suits
    Staggered DiD, Continuous treatment
    Corrected in v2.0. v2.0: pilot districts named. Source: Dawn, 16 December 2015.

    Rollout

    1. 2015PilotMalakand, Kohat, Mardan, Chitral. Dawn — Khattak launches health insurance scheme (16 Dec 2015)
    2. 2020Remaining districts in scope Dawn — KP govt launches Sehat Sahulat programme for all residents (20 Aug 2020)
    Where to get district-by-date data

    KP Health Department notifications; Sehat Card Plus programme history; KMUJ review

    Assessed quality: High. How to obtain timing data

    Identification

    Main threats

    The 2020 universalisation coincides with COVID; merged districts entered with the FATA merger

    Existing evidence

    Lancet Regional Health SEA commentary (2022); KMUJ review; no rigorous causal estimate published

    Power

    Constant 2019 boundaries in scope
    0.5 suits a not-yet-treated comparison
    AR(1) residual correlation
    —
    minimum detectable effect, in residual standard deviations

    Difference-in-differences variance with serially correlated errors (Burlig, Preonas and Woerman 2020), AR(1) residuals, two-group equivalent. Heterogeneity-robust staggered estimators usually need somewhat more.
