KP Sehat Card Plus (universal provincial coverage)

Four documented phases with expanding population and benefit coverage. Rolled out by district across 4 -> 24 -> all 35 incl. merged districts, 4 pilot districts (2015, poorest ~21%); ~24 districts (2017); all districts and 100% of population from 20 August 2020.

Status: Open  High timing data

Treatment timing

Where to get district-by-date data

KP Health Department notifications; Sehat Card Plus programme history; KMUJ review

Assessed quality: High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Two treatments in one: extensive margin (district entry) and intensive margin (share of population covered rising from 21% to 100%). A dose-response event study is feasible.

Main threats to identification

The 2020 universalisation coincides with COVID; merged districts entered with the FATA merger

Existing evidence

Lancet Regional Health SEA commentary (2022); KMUJ review; no rigorous causal estimate published
