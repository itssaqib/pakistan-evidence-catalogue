    P24   Education & skills

    PEF Education Vouchers

    PEF Education Voucher Scheme (EVS). Fifteen-plus expansion phases; phase 15 alone covered 16 districts (2015); reached 28+ districts.

    Overview

    Phase-level district lists with dates are published in the press and PEF reports - unusually easy treatment-timing data to assemble.

    Implementing agency
    Punjab Education Foundation
    Scope
    Punjab
    Rollout window
    Fifteen-plus expansion phases; phase 15 alone covered 16 districts (2015); reached 28+ districts
    Unit of rollout
    District (phase entry), household (voucher receipt)
    Type of staggering
    Repeated district-level expansion phases with named district lists
    Assignment rule
    Districts selected on out-of-school population and katchi abadi presence; households qualify on income
    Designs it suits
    Staggered DiD

    Rollout

    1. 2015Expansion phase 15Faisalabad, Jhang, Sheikhupura, Kasur, Okara, Rahim Yar Khan, Multan, Bahawalpur, Bahawalnagar, Khanewal, Rajanpur, Dera Ghazi Khan, Vehari, Lodhran, Muzaffargarh, Khushab. The News — PEF expands voucher scheme for students (22 Oct 2015)
    2. LaterFurther districtsLater phases reached more districts; their dates need the timing source below.
    Where to get district-by-date data

    PEF phase press releases naming districts and dates; PEF partner and voucher registers

    Assessed quality: High. How to obtain timing data

    Identification

    Main threats

    Vouchers target urban slums, so district is a coarse treatment unit; combine with settlement-level data

    Existing evidence

    Barrera-Osorio, Blakeslee, Hoover, Linden, Raju & Ryan on the rural school (NSP) experiment

    Data coverage

    Survey waves before, during and after the rollout window (2006–2015, shaded). Waves before the first phase identify pre-trends; waves inside the window carry the staggered comparison.

    Power

    Constant 2019 boundaries in scope
    0.5 suits a not-yet-treated comparison
    AR(1) residual correlation
    —
    minimum detectable effect, in residual standard deviations

    Difference-in-differences variance with serially correlated errors (Burlig, Preonas and Woerman 2020), AR(1) residuals, two-group equivalent. Heterogeneity-robust staggered estimators usually need somewhat more.
