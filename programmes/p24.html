PEF Education Voucher Scheme (EVS)

Repeated district-level expansion phases with named district lists. Rolled out by district across Grew to most of Punjab, fifteen-plus expansion phases; phase 15 alone covered 16 districts (2015); reached 28+ districts.

Treatment timing

Where to get district-by-date data

PEF phase press releases naming districts and dates; PEF partner and voucher registers

Assessed quality: High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Phase-level district lists with dates are published in the press and PEF reports - unusually easy treatment-timing data to assemble.

Main threats to identification

Vouchers target urban slums, so district is a coarse treatment unit; combine with settlement-level data

Existing evidence

Barrera-Osorio, Blakeslee, Hoover, Linden, Raju & Ryan on the rural school (NSP) experiment
