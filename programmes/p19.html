Lady Health Worker Programme coverage expansion

Continuous dose rather than sharp switch-on. Rolled out by district across All districts, varying intensity, continuous expansion; district coverage intensity varies through the 2000s and 2010s.

Treatment timing

Where to get district-by-date data

National Programme MIS; PSLM LHW visit module; third-party evaluations (OPM 2000, 2002, 2009)

Assessed quality: Medium. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Better treated as a continuous treatment (coverage share) than a binary event. The PSLM LHW module gives a household-reported visit measure that can validate administrative coverage.

Main threats to identification

Coverage is placed where female literacy allows recruitment - strongly endogenous to development

Existing evidence

OPM third-party evaluations; Wazir, Shaikh & Ahmed (2013)
