PEF New School Program (NSP)

Phase-in, with an embedded RCT in the 2008-2011 rural rounds. Rolled out by district across Subset of districts, phased across underserved districts and Cholistan; randomised in some rural rounds.

Treatment timing

Where to get district-by-date data

PEF NSP registers; the associated RCT documentation

Assessed quality: High for the RCT sample. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Where the RCT exists, use it. Elsewhere the village-level eligibility rule (no existing school within X km) supports a geographic RD.

Main threats to identification

Very local treatment - district DiD would badly dilute the effect

Existing evidence

Barrera-Osorio et al. (2020s) on public-private partnership school entry

Outcomes and data (4)

Each row pairs an outcome with the mechanism that would produce it, the expected direction, and a dataset that measures it at a compatible resolution. Expected signs are hypotheses, not findings.

IDOutcome Mechanism / hypothesis Expected Dataset Feasibility note
O-EDU-01 Enrolment in previously unserved villages Education A school appears where none existed +, large ASER Pakistan (Annual Status of Education Report); Pakistan Education Statistics / NEMIS; Agricultural Census & Mouza Census Distance to school is the binding constraint - expect large effects
O-EDU-07 Girls' enrolment specifically Education Distance binds much harder for girls + for girls, large ASER Pakistan (Annual Status of Education Report); Pakistan Education Statistics / NEMIS The strongest predicted heterogeneity in the whole catalogue
O-EDU-03 Learning levels Education New schools of variable quality +, uncertain ASER Pakistan (Annual Status of Education Report) The RCT rounds give internal validity
O-LAB-07 Child labour Labour School availability reduces work - (reduction) ASER Pakistan (Annual Status of Education Report); Labour Force Survey Village-level design needed
