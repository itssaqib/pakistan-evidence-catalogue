Identification

The KP two-phase 2021-22 local elections are close to an ideal natural experiment: same province, same rules, two dates a few months apart, sequencing driven by administrative rather than political criteria. Test it for balance.

Main threats to identification

Local government powers were repeatedly suspended and restored; devolved functions vary

Existing evidence

Some political science work; economics evidence is limited
