Sui gas network expansion

Staggered arrival of piped gas. Rolled out by district across Most districts, town and district connections notified over time; new connections were suspended nationally in 2021.

Where to get district-by-date data

SNGPL/SSGC scheme registers; OGRA annual reports; PSLM cooking-fuel module

Assessed quality: Medium. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

PSLM's cooking-fuel question gives a household-level verification of network arrival. Outcomes: fuel switching, indoor air pollution, female time use, firm energy costs.

Main threats to identification

Scheme allocation is famously political (a testable outcome in itself)

Existing evidence

Very thin

Outcomes and data (5)

Each row pairs an outcome with the mechanism that would produce it, the expected direction, and a dataset that measures it at a compatible resolution. Expected signs are hypotheses, not findings.
