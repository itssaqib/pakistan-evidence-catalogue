Where to get district-by-date data

HEC recognised institutions list with charter dates; provincial assembly acts; university first-intake records

Assessed quality: High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

'First university in the district' is a clean, dated, absorbing treatment. Outcomes: local tertiary enrolment, female enrolment (distance is a binding constraint for women), skilled wages, migration.

Main threats to identification

Campus placement responds to local political power and existing enrolment demand; use pre-trends and instrument with political alignment if defensible

Existing evidence

Thin - a substantial gap in the Pakistani literature
