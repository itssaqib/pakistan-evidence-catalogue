Identification

Because selection uses a literacy threshold, a fuzzy RD on the district female literacy rate complements the staggered DiD. This is one of the few Pakistani programmes with a near-deterministic assignment rule.

Main threats to identification

Targeting on low literacy generates mechanical mean reversion; overlaps with the older Punjab Female School Stipend Programme in several districts

Existing evidence

Related evidence: Chaudhury & Parajuli (2010) on the earlier Punjab stipend
