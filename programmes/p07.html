    Punjab Zewar-e-Taleem (girls' secondary stipend). Phased across 16-17 low-literacy districts, 2017-2019.

    Overview

    Because selection uses a literacy threshold, a fuzzy RD on the district female literacy rate complements the staggered DiD. This is one of the few Pakistani programmes with a near-deterministic assignment rule.

    Implementing agency
    Punjab Social Protection Authority
    Scope

    Rollout window

    Unit of rollout
    District
    Type of staggering
    Discrete district set, entering in phases
    Assignment rule
    Selected on low female literacy rate - a deterministic rule on a running variable
    Designs it suits
    Staggered DiD, Regression discontinuity

    Identification

    Main threats

    Targeting on low literacy generates mechanical mean reversion; overlaps with the older Punjab Female School Stipend Programme in several districts

    Existing evidence

    Related evidence: Chaudhury & Parajuli (2010) on the earlier Punjab stipend

    Power

    Constant 2019 boundaries in scope
    From the documented district list
    AR(1) residual correlation
    —
    minimum detectable effect, in residual standard deviations

    Difference-in-differences variance with serially correlated errors (Burlig, Preonas and Woerman 2020), AR(1) residuals, two-group equivalent. Heterogeneity-robust staggered estimators usually need somewhat more.
