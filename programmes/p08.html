Punjab Female School Stipend Programme (PFSSP)

One-shot with a sharp assignment rule, plus later extension waves. Rolled out by district across 15 of 34, 15 low-literacy districts from 2004; later extension.

Assessed quality: High. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

The canonical Pakistani regression-discontinuity-in-district-characteristics design. Still under-exploited for long-run outcomes (marriage age, fertility, labour supply of the treated cohorts, now in their 30s).

Main threats to identification

Only 34 districts existed in 1998 - very few observations near the cut-off; district splits after 2005 complicate the panel

Existing evidence

Chaudhury & Parajuli (2010, Applied Economics); Alam, Baez & Del Carpio (2011)
