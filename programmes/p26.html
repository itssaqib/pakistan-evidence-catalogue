Punjab Education Sector Reform Programme (PESRP) and the school reform roadmap

Province-wide policy with district-varying implementation intensity. Rolled out by district across 36, pESRP I 2003-2009, II 2009-2015, III 2016-2020; roadmap and delivery unit from 2011.

Treatment timing

Where to get district-by-date data

Punjab Monitoring & Implementation Unit records; district league tables; PMIU dashboards

Assessed quality: Medium. Verify against the primary document before use — dates in press coverage and programme retrospectives frequently disagree by a year. How to obtain timing data →

Identification

Not a clean on/off treatment. Best used as a continuous 'monitoring intensity' measure interacted with the roadmap start, or as a context control for other education programmes.

Main threats to identification

Province-wide timing gives no untreated comparison group inside Punjab; use KP or Sindh as controls with caution

Existing evidence

Barber (2013) practitioner account; Callen, Gulzar, Hasanain & Khan work on monitoring
