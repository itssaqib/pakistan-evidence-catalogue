What this is for

Pakistan ran an unusual number of large public programmes that reached districts at different times: health insurance activated division by division, land records digitised tehsil by tehsil, cash transfers gated by the date a district's poverty census finished. That staggering is the variation causal inference needs, and most of it has never been used.

The binding constraint is rarely the idea. It is knowing when each district was treated, and whether an outcome exists at the same resolution. This catalogue answers both questions for each entry, and says plainly where the answer is weak.
