    Treatment dates

    The binding constraint on most of these ideas is knowing which district was treated when. Nine routes, roughly in order of reliability.

    MethodsTreatment datesCaveats
    The practical order

    Start with published evaluations and World Bank implementation reports: someone may already have built the file. Then Annual Development Programme scheme registers, which are public and under-used. Then a Right to Information request naming a specific register. Treat press reports as triangulation only.

    SourceWhat it gives youWherePractical note
    Provincial gazette notificationsThe legally authoritative record of when a district was created, when a law was extended, when a facility was notified. Punjab, Sindh, KP and Balochistan each publish a gazette; back issues are held by provincial law departments and the Punjab series is partly digitised.punjablaws.gov.pkSlow but definitive. Use it to settle disagreements between secondary sources, not as your first stop.
    Annual Development Programme scheme registersEvery publicly funded scheme — a rescue station, a road package, a filtration plant, a hospital upgrade — appears as a line item with a district, an approval date and a completion status. This is the most under-used treatment-timing source in Pakistan.pnd.punjab.gov.pkPublished annually as PDFs. Scraping ten years of ADP books yields a district–scheme–date panel for dozens of programmes at once.
    PC-1 approval and PC-4 completion documentsThe PC-1 states the intended district phasing; the PC-4 records what was actually completed and when. Held by the Planning Commission and provincial P&D departments.pc.gov.pkRequest through the department. The PC-4 is the one that matters, because plans slip.
    Right to Information requestsKP (2013), Punjab (2013), Sindh (2016) and Balochistan (2021) all have RTI statutes with statutory response deadlines. A request naming the exact register you want — for example, the date of operationalisation of each Arazi Record Centre — is often the fastest route to a clean treatment-timing file.infocom.punjab.gov.pkAsk for a specific existing record, not for analysis. Requests that ask an official to compile something new are usually refused.
    Programme management information systemsBISP/NSER, Sehat Sahulat, the Punjab Land Records Authority, PITB, the Punjab Education Foundation, PSDF, and the provincial EMIS and DHIS systems all hold unit-level records with dates. Access runs through a research agreement or data-sharing MoU, not a public download.See the dataset catalogueA World Bank, J-PAL or university affiliation materially improves the odds. Budget three to six months.
    World Bank and ADB project documentsProject Appraisal Documents, numbered Implementation Status Reports and Implementation Completion Reports record the actual district phasing of donor-financed programmes, often in an annex.documents.worldbank.orgThe ISR sequence is especially useful: successive reports show the rollout progressing, which effectively date-stamps it.
    Third-party and process evaluationsEvaluation series and published papers frequently contain the district rollout table as an appendix. Malik, van de Poel & van Doorslaer (2017) is the clearest example: they assembled the health-unit contracting rollout dates across 75 districts.opml.co.ukAlways check the replication package before assembling a rollout file from scratch.
    Press archivesDawn, The News, Express Tribune and Business Recorder report launch ceremonies with dates and district lists. Some programme phases are documented almost exclusively this way.dawn.com/archiveUseful for triangulation, unreliable alone — a launch ceremony is not an operational start.
    Satellite and crowd-sourced verificationWhere administrative dates are unavailable or untrustworthy, nightlights, OpenStreetMap edit history and mobile coverage polygons can date the arrival of infrastructure independently.eogdata.mines.eduBest used to validate an administrative date rather than to replace it.

    An RTI request that works

    Ask for a record that already exists, not for analysis. Name the register, the custodian and the fields.

    To: Public Information Officer, [Department]
Under Section 10, Punjab Transparency and
Right to Information Act 2013

I request a copy of the following existing record:

1. The register or database recording the date on
   which each [facility / centre / scheme] under
   [programme] became operational, listing district,
   tehsil, unit name and date of operationalisation.

2. Covering the period [start] to [end].

I am content to receive it in any electronic format
in which it is already held. I do not require any
new compilation or analysis.

    How timing data is graded

    High
    Dates are published, or sit in a named register someone has already assembled. Verifying takes hours.
    Medium-high
    Dates exist in administrative records and can be obtained with a request or a document search. Weeks.
    Medium
    Dates exist but are scattered across sources that disagree. Expect to reconcile and document judgement calls.
    Low
    No reliable district dating known. Needs primary collection or a satellite proxy.
